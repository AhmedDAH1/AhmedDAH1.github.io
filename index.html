SOAR-Lite

Automating the SOC triage loop, end to end.

From webhook alert to finished incident report — without the manual copy-paste.

SOC analysts lose hours to repetitive triage: checking whether an IP is malicious, escalating incidents by hand, pasting indicators into reports. SOAR-Lite runs that chain automatically — ingest, extract, enrich, decide, act, document — and cuts simulated response time from 30+ minutes to under two.

  • IngestionWebhooks from SIEM, EDR, and email-gateway formats
  • ExtractionRegex IOC parsing — IPs, domains, hashes, emails
  • EnrichmentAsync VirusTotal & AbuseIPDB lookups
  • AutomationYAML playbook engine auto-escalates & tags
  • OutputOne-click PDF / DOCX executive reports
  • Verification53 tests · 84% coverage · GitHub Actions
